Beautiful.ai guards design in a SaaS editor. presentation-md generates decks inside the agent you already use.

Where Beautiful.ai is stronger

  • Smart templates that resist layout collapse while you edit
  • Team brand kits and designer-led polish in a guided UI
  • Collaboration and sharing inside a familiar SaaS product

Where presentation-md is stronger

  • Runs inside Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Codex, Gemini CLI — no tab switch
  • Portable HTML + native PPTX with speaker notes — PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides
  • Theme-native PPTX craft (hard cards, frames, audits) — agents ship editable decks, not flat exports
  • 75 schema-validated themes, asymmetric craft, MCP tools, brand import
  • Free, MIT licensed, no account or subscription

Verdict

If you want a polished SaaS editor with smart templates and brand kits, Beautiful.ai is a strong product. If your decks should come from the AI agent already in your terminal or IDE — with a file you own and no platform lock-in — presentation-md is the shorter path.
